Peeky: Pocket Sidekick

Privacy Policy

Peeky keeps your notes, plans, and payment reminders on your device. If you turn on iCloud, they also sync privately through your own Apple Account. The developer never receives them.

Last updated: October 23, 2026. The notebook audio features below apply to Peeky 1.2 and later.

Summary

  • Peeky does not require an account and does not operate its own server.
  • Peeky does not use advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • Your content stays on your device, and — when you are signed in to iCloud — in your own private iCloud storage. The developer cannot read it.
  • Optional analytics and diagnostics stay on your device and are never sent automatically.

Information processed by Peeky

Notes, plans, reminders, and payment reminders

Content you enter, including note text, checklists, reminder titles, calendar plans, future messages, payment labels, amounts, due dates, and the record of payments you mark as paid, is stored so the app can provide its features. Peeky does not send this content to the developer.

Photos, files, and drawings you add to a note

You can place photos and files on a note's canvas and draw on it with Apple Pencil or your finger. Those images, documents, and ink strokes are stored inside Peeky's private app storage on your device and are treated exactly like your note text. Peeky uses the system photo picker, which hands over only the items you pick, so Peeky never requests access to your whole photo library.

iCloud sync

If your device is signed in to iCloud, Peeky mirrors your notes, attachments, drawings, plans, and payment reminders into the private database of your own iCloud account (container iCloud.com.peeky.app) so your devices stay in step. This storage belongs to you and is governed by Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy. The developer has no access to it and receives no copy. If you are not signed in to iCloud, or you turn iCloud off for Peeky in iOS Settings, everything simply stays on the device.

Voice notes and transcription

Capture turns microphone audio into text for you to review and save. Capture does not retain a separate audio file. In Peeky 1.2 and later, transcription requires Apple's on-device Speech recognition; if a supported language model is unavailable, Peeky reports that transcription is unavailable rather than sending the recording to a remote transcription service.

Notebook recording is separate from Capture. When you record while writing or drawing, Peeky saves an audio file as an attachment to the note, together with timing information that links your ink strokes to the recording. The audio, timing information and any resulting transcript are stored in the app's private storage and can sync through your private iCloud account when iCloud sync is enabled. A pending recording may be kept locally for recovery until it is attached successfully. Transcription is attempted after recording stops; unavailable transcription does not remove the saved audio.

Handwriting recognition uses Apple's Vision framework on your device. Recognized text and its page locations can be saved with the note for editing, search and actions you confirm. The developer receives none of your recordings, drawings, transcripts or recognized text unless you explicitly share them.

Calendar and Reminders

With your permission, Peeky reads the events and incomplete reminders scheduled for the day you are viewing and shows them, read-only, alongside your own plans. Peeky also creates calendar events and Apple Reminders when you ask it to. This information is read on the device for display; Peeky does not copy it to the developer.

Locked notes and Face ID

You can seal a note so it can only be opened after Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. Authentication is performed by iOS; Peeky is only told whether it succeeded and never sees your biometric data. Sealed notes are excluded from system search indexing.

System search (Spotlight)

So you can find a note from iOS search, Peeky adds note titles and a short excerpt to the on-device Spotlight index. Sealed notes and notes in the paper basket are excluded. This index is maintained by iOS on your device.

Content shared into Peeky

When you use the iOS share sheet to send text, a link, or an image to Peeky, that item is handed to Peeky by the system and becomes a note. Peeky receives only what you explicitly share.

Siri, Shortcuts, and the Action button

Peeky offers actions such as capturing a note or opening the payment radar. When you dictate to Siri, Apple processes your speech under Apple's terms and gives Peeky the resulting text. Peeky stores that text as a note on your device.

Widgets, Lock Screen, and Dynamic Island

To draw its widgets and Live Activities, Peeky writes a small summary — such as your next plan's title and time, the next payment's title and amount, and simple counts — into a shared storage area on your device that only Peeky and its own extensions can read. Buttons on those surfaces record your tap locally and apply it the next time you open the app.

Optional local analytics and diagnostics

If you opt in, Peeky can store basic feature events and technical crash information locally on your device. These records do not include note text, transcripts, payment details, or calendar content. They are not automatically sent to the developer. You can turn these options off and delete stored diagnostics in Settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Peeky receives verified entitlement information needed to unlock the app. The developer does not receive your payment card or bank details.

App access verification

Peeky uses Apple's signed AppTransaction record to determine the original App Store acquisition date and provide one 7-day full-access period. The signed acquisition date does not reset when the app is deleted and reinstalled. Peeky also stores a local Keychain timestamp on the device to protect the access period from clock rollback. This access data is not sent to the developer.

Device permissions

  • Microphone: used when you start Capture or a notebook recording.
  • Speech Recognition: used to turn speech into text through Apple's Speech framework. Peeky 1.2 requires a supported on-device model.
  • Calendar: used to show the day's events on your plan board and to add events you ask for.
  • Reminders: used to show reminders due that day and to create the ones you ask for.
  • Face ID: used only to unlock notes you chose to seal.
  • Notifications: used for alerts you choose, such as plan times and upcoming payments.
  • Live Activities: used to show the companion, a focus guard, or your day's progress on supported Lock Screen and Dynamic Island surfaces.

You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings. Core manual note features remain available if you decline optional permissions.

Sharing and third parties

Peeky does not sell personal information, serve ads, or share your content with data brokers. Apple may process information when you use Apple services such as iCloud, Speech Recognition, Siri, Calendar, Reminders, notifications, Live Activities, and App Store purchases. Apple's handling of that information is governed by Apple's terms and privacy policies.

When you choose to share something from inside Peeky — a note card, a weekly receipt image, a data export, or a beta feedback report — the iOS share sheet lets you pick the destination, and the content goes only where you send it. Feedback reports contain app and device diagnostics but exclude your notes, transcripts, payment details, and calendar content.

Retention and deletion

Local app content remains on your device until you delete the item or remove the app. Deleted notes, plans, and payments first move to Peeky's paper basket, where they can be restored for 30 days before being removed for good; you can also empty the basket immediately.

If iCloud sync is on, deletions travel to your other devices in the same way. Removing the app from a device deletes its local copy; content that was synced remains in your iCloud account until it is deleted from a device that is still signed in, or through iCloud storage settings.

Optional local diagnostics can be deleted from Peeky Settings at any time. Calendar events and Apple Reminders created at your request are managed in the corresponding Apple apps and remain there until you delete them.

Because Peeky does not require an account and keeps no copy of your app content on a developer server, there is no server account for the developer to delete.

Children's privacy

Peeky is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed specifically to children. The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a Peeky-operated server.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Peeky's features or data practices change. The updated date at the top of this page identifies the latest version.
