Peeky: Pocket Sidekick

Privacy Policy

Peeky is designed as a local-first app. Your notes, plans, transcripts, and payment reminders stay on your device.

Last updated: July 13, 2026

Summary

  • Peeky does not require an account.
  • Peeky does not use advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • Peeky does not operate an analytics, transcription, or cloud storage server in this version.
  • Your content and optional diagnostics are stored locally on your device.

Information processed by Peeky

Notes, plans, reminders, and payment reminders

Content you enter, including note text, reminder titles, calendar plans, future messages, payment labels, amounts, and due dates, is stored locally so the app can provide its features. Peeky does not send this content to the developer.

Voice notes and transcription

When you start a voice note, Peeky sends microphone audio to Apple's Speech framework for the transcription you request. Depending on your device, language, and Apple's service availability, processing may occur on the device or through Apple's services. Peeky does not create or retain a separate audio file and the developer does not receive your audio or transcript.

Optional local analytics and diagnostics

If you opt in, Peeky can store basic feature events and technical crash information locally on your device. These records do not include note text, transcripts, payment details, or calendar content. They are not automatically sent to the developer. You can turn these options off and delete stored diagnostics in Settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Peeky receives verified entitlement information needed to unlock the app. The developer does not receive your payment card or bank details.

App access verification

Peeky uses Apple's signed AppTransaction record to determine the original App Store acquisition date and provide one 7-day full-access period. The signed acquisition date does not reset when the app is deleted and reinstalled. Peeky also stores a local Keychain timestamp on the device to protect the access period from clock rollback. This access data is not sent to the developer.

Device permissions

  • Microphone: used only while you start and run a voice note.
  • Speech Recognition: used to turn a voice note into text through Apple's Speech framework.
  • Calendar: used only when you ask Peeky to add a calendar event.
  • Reminders: used only when you ask Peeky to add an Apple Reminder.
  • Notifications: used for alerts you choose, such as reminders and upcoming payments.
  • Live Activities: used to show the selected companion and status on supported Lock Screen and Dynamic Island surfaces.

You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings. Core manual note features remain available if you decline optional permissions.

Sharing and third parties

Peeky does not sell personal information, serve ads, or share your content with data brokers. Apple may process information when you use Apple services such as Speech Recognition, Calendar, Reminders, notifications, Live Activities, and App Store purchases. Apple's handling of that information is governed by Apple's terms and privacy policies.

If you choose to share a beta feedback report, the iOS share sheet lets you select the destination. The report contains app and device diagnostics but excludes your notes, transcripts, payment details, and calendar content.

Retention and deletion

Local app content remains on your device until you delete the item or remove the app. Optional local diagnostics can be deleted from Peeky Settings at any time. Calendar events and Apple Reminders created at your request are managed in the corresponding Apple apps and remain there until you delete them.

Because Peeky does not require an account and does not keep your app content on a developer server, there is no server account or cloud content for the developer to delete.

Children's privacy

Peeky is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed specifically to children. The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a Peeky-operated server.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Peeky's features or data practices change. The updated date at the top of this page identifies the latest version.
